# The Last Address: 27-map layout audit

Layout revision 4. These are original, authored circulation plans with randomized POI contents. All original large footprints remain. The previous six-node permutations have been replaced with 12–14 spatial nodes per world, separated task districts and three named landmark courts.

Research: [Jared Pike — Dream Pools](https://www.jaredpike.art/projects/dream-pools/) explores familiar pool architecture and eerie nostalgia. [Dreamcore’s official environment descriptions](https://www.dreamcoregame.com/) show the value of connected ramps, stairs, contrasting chambers and uncanny domestic spaces. [Claire Hosking’s GDC workshop](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1023554/Level-Design-Workshop-Architecture-in) describes mood through geometry, material, lighting and construction. This implementation applies those principles to original world-specific plans; it does not reproduce their assets.

The home estate has exactly 27 unique destination houses, three public landmarks, garden mews, service lanes and a composed opening sidewalk. Unioned road/sidewalk patches remove overlapping junction slabs. Ordinary doors begin closed and unlocked; the mansion retains its chained gate.

Extracted from the actual built game, campaign 4919. Coordinates are metres, Y up, X east and Z south. The atlas contains ground-floor SVG exports plus selectable elevations, collision slices, floor voids, routes, controls, doors and discovery approaches. It does not reveal unexplored maps inside the game.
